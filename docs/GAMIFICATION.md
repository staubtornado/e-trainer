# Progression rules v1

The pure Kotlin engine takes canonical journal records, effective policies, exceptions, goal snapshots and an injected local date. Outputs are deterministic sets of credit days, awards, milestones, levels and forecast evidence. Rules below are product parameters, not training prescriptions. A session always belongs in the journal even if it earns zero points.

## Calendar and qualifying events

Use the profile’s fixed IANA scoring zone, seeded on first use. Persist civil date at entry (session start, meal time) plus original instant/zone. Moving between time zones does not move a historical credit. Correcting the date is an explicit edit. Weeks are ISO Monday–Sunday; today and future cutoffs use the injected clock. Future logs earn nothing until their day arrives. Never award for drafts, weights lifted, duration beyond qualification, calories, deficits or weight changes.

- A training candidate is a distinct day with a deliberately finished session and at least one completed work set (positive reps or timed seconds). Warmups alone do not qualify. A meal candidate has at least one saved meal with a positive confirmed portion and an item. Nutrition completeness is irrelevant to credit.
- A dated exception (rest, illness, travel or personal) neutralizes that day for both streams. It can be added/removed retroactively with a preview. Preferred recovery weekdays in the effective policy are neutral automatically. Keep logs visible. No credit, negative XP, financial effect or need to make up the day.
- Per week let E be the count of non-neutral calendar dates on/after enrollment. Effective training quota T = min(preferred frequency, E), frequency is an integer 1–4. Meal quota N = min(3,E). Credit the earliest T distinct training candidate dates and earliest N distinct meal candidate dates among eligible dates. At most one credit per type per day. Sort by date; compute for each stream independently. A backdated log can move which dates receive credit.
- Each credited training day earns 10 XP; each credited meal day earns 5. All wishes share this ledger. XP = sum of current awards. Level = 1 + floor(XP / 100); next level threshold = 100 × current level. Achievements add no XP. No negative ledger entries or decay. Corrections can reduce displayed totals.

## Wishes and personalized targets

A wish selects one stream, one real-world description and one difficulty. Its progress counts globally credited dates of that type from its start date inclusive. Multiple wishes can use the same date. Starting midweek cannot bypass the global cap. Starts default to today and cannot precede enrollment. The activation preview shows frequency, neutral weekdays, evidence coverage, base rate, horizon, proposed total and the option to choose an explicit integer target 1–365. Store all inputs and the accepted target.

1. Compute plan rate P = min(stream’s nominal weekly quota, 7 − number of distinct scheduled recovery weekdays). Require at least one available day for activation. Use the current schedule. Weekly quotas above already account for dated exceptions.
2. Look back over the latest four **closed** ISO weeks. For calibration use only whole weeks after enrollment with no neutral dates beyond recurring rest weekdays and at least one available day. Include zero-credit weeks. A week starting on enrollment Monday counts as whole. If at least two usable weeks exist, B = clamp(mean(credited days across usable weeks), 1, P). Otherwise B = P, labeled “based on your plan”. Keep the unrounded rational until the final step.
3. Gentle, standard and extended use horizons H = 4, 8 and 12 weeks. Offer target ceil(B × H). Difficulty changes the duration of the challenge. Weekly caps stay fixed. Show checkpoints at the sorted unique values ceil(target × f) for f = ¼, ½, ¾ and 1.
4. Example: preferred training frequency 3, four eligible weeks with 2, 3, 2, 3 credits. B = 2.5. The standard television wish proposes **20 training days**. After 10 credited dates the halfway checkpoint lights up. If the same four-week pace still applies, the remaining 10 days imply about 4 weeks.
5. On the first visit after a week closes, recalculate the preview for **new** wishes. For an active training wish offer a schedule review only after two consecutive complete eligible weeks below P. Evaluate only the training stream, using each week’s historical cap and the current schedule for the proposed change. Suggest max(1, floor(mean of those two counts)) capped at current P; suppress if unchanged. Store dismissal per week. Show previous and proposed policy; approval creates a revision effective next Monday. No automatic raising, retroactive rescaling or loss of earned credit from a future plan change. A different accepted target starts a new wish; archive the previous one with its history.

## Streaks, forecasting and redemption

A training consistency streak counts consecutive successful **closed** weeks. Determine the result in chronological order. Enrollment’s partial week and weeks with E = 0 are neutral. For other weeks, reaching T is success; increment the streak. A shortfall in a week with any dated exception is neutral (hold the streak); other shortfalls reset it. Recurring recovery weekdays simply reduce E. On the first day of a new week the previous week is evaluated even if the app was closed at midnight. A current week shows “in progress” with credited count and quota. Record the best closed-week streak by replay. The first credited training day, first credited meal day, fourth successful week in a streak and first fully credited wish are achievement conditions. Deduplicate by achievement code per profile. Derive the earliest evidence date; do not retrigger an animation on rotation.

Forecast each active wish from actual credited days of its stream in the same eligible closed-week sample as calibration. Require at least two usable weeks and a positive raw mean R. Remaining = max(0, target − progress). Report ceil(remaining / min(R,P)) weeks if P > 0, explicitly an estimate at recent pace. Evaluate in order: zero remaining yields “target reached”; no upcoming eligible dates in the next seven days yields “paused”; missing R yields “insufficient history”. For a nonzero positive rate use the estimate above. The denominator here includes zero weeks and has no floor of one. The result is a coarse duration, not a promised date.

Status is active, archived or claimed. Readiness for an active wish is computed from current evidence. Claim requires readiness and confirmation, then stores a unique receipt with claim time and target/progress snapshot. Editing old evidence rebuilds XP and current counts, while a historic claim stays visible with its receipt. Stop counting progress at claim/archive date for closed wishes. For first fully credited wish evaluate cumulative progress by date up to that cutoff. Archive by explicit command only.

Optional savings use manual signed deposits/withdrawals in one ISO currency. Show recorded balance and optional price on a separate line. Require positive amounts in the UI, explicit direction and a nonnegative resulting balance across dated transactions. No exchange rates, automatic deposits, cash value for XP or requirement to have the money to mark a symbolic milestone.

## Replay contract

Persist rule version 1 on policy, wish snapshots and derived evidence. Rebuild from journal plus policy history on finish/edit/delete, date change, exception, import and recovery. Use unique keys (credit type, day) and (achievement code). Replace affected results atomically. Full replay is the reference oracle. Rules upgrades keep old effective versions for old dates. Display the exact reasons for a zero (“weekly quota reached”, “recovery day”, “draft”) and allow navigation to the underlying records.
